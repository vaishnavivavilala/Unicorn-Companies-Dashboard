# Unicorn-Companies-Dashboard
This project analyzes global unicorn companies to uncover trends in valuation, industries, funding, and geographic distribution. The dashboard provides interactive insights into how startups grow into billion-dollar companies.

.

📁 Dataset Details

The dataset includes the following key fields:
Company Name
Valuation
Date Joined (when company became unicorn)
Industry
City, Country, Continent
Year Founded
Funding Amount
Investors

🎯 Objectives

Analyze the growth of unicorn companies over time
Identify top industries producing unicorns
Understand geographic distribution of startups
Compare funding vs valuation trends
Highlight key investors and their impact

📊 Key Insights

🚀 Tech and FinTech industries dominate unicorn creation
🌍 Majority of unicorns are concentrated in USA, China, and India
📈 Significant growth in unicorns after 2015
💰 Higher funding often correlates with higher valuation
🏙️ Cities like San Francisco, Beijing, and Bangalore are major startup hubs

📌 Dashboard Features
Interactive filters (Industry, Country, Year)
KPI cards (Total Unicorns, Total Funding, Avg Valuation)

Visualizations:
Bar charts (Top industries & countries)
Line charts (Unicorn growth over years)
Maps (Geographic distribution)
Pie/Donut charts (Industry share)

🛠 Tools Used
Power BI (Data Visualization)
Data Cleaning & Transformation (Power Query)

📈 Conclusion
This dashboard helps in understanding global startup trends, identifying high-growth sectors, and analyzing investment patterns. It can be useful for investors, analysts, and business strategists.
